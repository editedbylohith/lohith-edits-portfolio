About

Crafting stories,
frame by frame.

I'm Lohith Gadam — a video editor and post-production artist based in Maharashtra, India.

My approach is simple: story first, always. Whether it's a 30-second spot or a feature-length project, I obsess over pacing, tone, and the invisible cuts that make a narrative feel effortless. I believe great editing should be felt, not seen.

When I'm not in the timeline, you'll find me analyzing cinematic visual effects in Marvel films and discovering innovative storytelling techniques across YouTube.
